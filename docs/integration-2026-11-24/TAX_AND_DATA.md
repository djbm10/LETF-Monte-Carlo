# Tax, data and execution scope

Reviewed 2026-11-24. These are bounded calculation rules, not a complete individual return or a prediction of future tax law. The public APIs reject unsupported years, states and instrument structures rather than silently using California or 2024 parameters.

MA is restricted to a full-year resident with wage income sufficient to use the stated personal exemption and ordinary corporate-security income. No-tax-status/limited-income credits, other deductions, pension exclusions, residency changes, tax-exempt/government-obligation passthrough and special gains are not implemented. TX/FL/NV are modeled only as states with no general personal investment-income tax; federal-only must be chosen explicitly when appropriate. No default state is assumed.

Known-year tables cover 2024–2026. Earlier historical household returns are blocked. The future scenario runner deliberately freezes an explicitly selected tax year and reports `HYPOTHETICAL_STRESS`; this is not future enacted law. The MA source module includes rates/statutory rules through the stated years; no complete 2026 filing form is claimed.

The taxable daily engine pays **incremental household investment tax** relative to the same ordinary-income baseline. A negative amount is a tax benefit; it becomes settled cash only on a supplied refund/payment date. Terminal net wealth includes accrued liability/receivable and reports unsettled security-sale proceeds separately. Annual April payment assumptions require sufficient outside withholding; estimated-tax safe harbors/penalties are not independently computed. Tax-funded sales produce new current-year realizations. Loss observation windows must close, with explicit owner-wide information or a supplied no-external-replacements assumption.

Roth contribution history is separate from portfolio investment income. The demo supplies existing regular basis; recurring contributions validate MAGI and compensation. Conversions require a supplied taxable amount from the owner's pro-rata/Form 8606 analysis and report external personal taxable income; they are not routine Roth capital gains. Taxable/penalized withdrawals requiring a personal-tax payment schedule reject in the daily runner. Inherited IRAs, special rollover clocks, excess corrections, UBTI/UBIT and prohibited-transaction tax consequences are not modeled. The ledger rejects noncorporate security tax structures rather than assuming ordinary ETF taxation.

## Sources and conventions

BRK-B capture: Yahoo/yfinance 0.2.66, timestamp 2026-11-24T12:14:39.662745+00:00; 752 daily rows, first 2023-01-03, last 2025-12-31, USD. OHLC/close are as-traded only after checking all available actions through 2026-11-23, no splits/distributions and agreement with adjusted close. [Manifest](evidence/account-input-manifest.json) records exact SHA-256/provider/source URLs and vendor usage restrictions. Issuer annual reports are linked in the main report. This single surviving-stock example does not establish a survivor-free stock-selection study.

ETF capture reuses the original immutable November 23 daily snapshots; source hashes are checked before any transformation. Dated 2024 ProShares ICI primary data has a report date of 2024-12-31 and target delivery January 21, 2025. It is appropriate for ex-post tax classification, and never enters signal histories. The current [issuer API](https://www.proshares.com/api/distributionsummary/?fund=TQQQ&year=2024) returns split-restated values; these are compared with the dated original and with actual recorded split actions. TQQQ/QLD agree on a constant factor of 2 across all four distributions. Their vendor cash payouts were rounded; final cash distributions come from the dated official record. A first attempt to infer a constant factor from the rounded vendor payout amounts **failed** the unchanged 2e−5 consistency tolerance; the original build artifact is retained locally. The second method uses exact issuer records; the tolerance was not relaxed. No in-period split is silently handled by that builder.

Investor qualification is distinct from fund-designated QDI. The daily taxable engine accepts source-classified qualified distributions only with already-established backward holding-period evidence; holdings acquired within 60 days reject because the remaining 121-day-window treatment is not inferred. This conservative gate can block an ultimately qualifying dividend. Callers need a separate verified forward eligibility/reclassification schedule to expand that scope.

CPI: BLS CPIAUCNS via FRED, monthly NSA levels, current retrieved vintage. Mapped to observation month only for ex-post endpoint metrics, never strategy features. October 2025 is missing, as documented by [BLS](https://www.bls.gov/cpi/additional-resources/2025-federal-government-shutdown-impact-cpi-faq.htm). There is no interpolation or assertion of historical release-vintage availability. Raw snapshots stay local under `audit-output`; metadata and derived results are committed. Redistribution rights to vendor quote files are not certified.

Reconstruction: daily adjusted fund/benchmark returns from frozen vendor files; ACT/365 fees, ACT/360 borrowing using calendar intervals; DFF shifted by two calendar days is a proxy, not a swap invoice. Products use constant fee scenarios; historical waivers/spreads are not verified. Actual adjusted fund returns are passed through at leverage one with no internal fee/funding subtraction. Path decay emerges from compounding. Source data frequency, internal gaps, nonfinite values and closure rules are validated. The historical input module is distinct from the mathematical scenario generator.

## Derivatives and brokerage evidence

CME contract math in tests uses specified tick/multiplier formulas; e.g. [MES specifications](https://www.cmegroup.com/markets/equities/sp/micro-e-mini-sandp-500.contractSpecs.html) identify a $5 index multiplier and 0.25-point tick. Scenario margins are labeled as scenario amounts, not invented CME historical schedules. No independent dated exchange tape is captured. The research dated adapter preserves distinct contracts and charges both sides of rolls; it does not replace unknown intraday fills with a continuous-chart observation. Negative futures prices are possible and supported by the book; the NQ-specific positive-price research sizing rule does not generalize to negative commodity contracts.

The generic Roth cash profile allows ordinary stock/ETF longs, no borrowing, short writing or futures. The dated [IBKR IRA FAQ](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/support/faq/content/1136195127) and [Fidelity limited-margin agreement](https://www.fidelity.com/bin-public/060_www_fidelity_com/documents/limited-margin-agreement.pdf) support limited conservative broker subsets in `letf/brokers.py`; personal options/futures approval, dated house collateral and account minimums remain required inputs. Current documents do not establish permission in an earlier historical year. Limited margin is not authority to borrow without restriction.

SPX sample contract definitions use the supplied exercise-style field and [Cboe cash/European/$100 specifications](https://www.cboe.com/tradable-products/sp-500/spx-options/spx-specifications/). A public sample date is not fabricated into a quote timestamp or depth. AM/PM official settlement values are not interchangeable with underlying close. LongOptionBook requires supplied dated contract-matched bid/ask/depth for orders; expiry requires explicit official settlement input. Physical ITM exercise, adjusted OCC deliverables, short assignment and taxable hedge interactions reject. Straddle/covered-call/constructive-sale provisions (IRC 1092/1259/1234) are not assumed away by the collateral helpers.
