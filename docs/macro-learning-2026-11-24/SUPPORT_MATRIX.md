# Supported combinations and evidence gates

`OBSERVED` describes source observations, not guaranteed fills. A
`VALIDATED_RECONSTRUCTION` requires both numerical and provenance gates; **none
of the five ETF reconstructions has that full designation**. `HYPOTHETICAL_STRESS`
means explicit scenario assumptions. `BLOCKED` means required data/mechanics are
absent or a confidence gate failed. Passing fixture mechanics is not historical
investment certification.

Generic Roth permits stock/ETF cash longs and cannot silently obtain unrestricted
margin. An expressly approved broker profile may enable a bounded derivative
subset; statutory IRA status alone is insufficient. Unsupported years, tax
structures, permissions, collateral, source gaps and executions fail explicitly.
User-supplied false provenance flags cannot establish factual evidence.

Further engineering gaps include automatic TLH, per-order specific-lot selection
in the strategy CLI, forward QDI reclassification, full historical tax laws,
estimated-tax penalties and derivative-funded personal tax schedules. FIFO/HIFO
and explicit specific identification exist in the lower-level ledger. No complete
capability is inferred solely from a helper or a passing unit count.
